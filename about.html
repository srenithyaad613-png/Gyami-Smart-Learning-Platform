ABOUT GYAMI

Learning, made more human. ✦

Gyami is a student-focused smart learning platform designed to bring everyday study tools into one simple space.

🎯 Purpose

Make study planning, practice and progress tracking easier for students.

⚡ Built for Action

Notes, focus sessions, quizzes, assignments and progress work together instead of living in separate places.

🌱 Future Ready

The structure can grow into richer AI tutoring, recommendations and campus learning tools.
